# cape-town-day

The Akhanya base runs three concurrent tracks: AKHARA (the agency, the whales), PPL (the school, the discipline), Prop (the prop firm trading, the cashflow practice). Most days one track dominates. Most weeks all three should advance.

This skill produces the morning's plan for all three, in one page, in five minutes.

## The contract

Output, in this order:

```
CAPE TOWN — [day, date] · SAST

WEATHER + ENERGY
- [actual or estimated weather]
- [user's energy on 1-10 scale]

AKHARA · today's one action
- [single specific action]
- est: [Xm]   window: [HH:MM SAST]
- done state: [what success looks like]

PPL · today's one action
- [single specific action]
- est: [Xm]   window: [HH:MM SAST]
- done state: [what success looks like]

PROP · today's one action
- [single specific action]
- est: [Xm]   window: [HH:MM SAST]
- done state: [what success looks like]

CROSS-TRACK NOTE
- [The single coupling between today's actions. Often "trading window first, so AKHARA work comes after market close" or "PPL session aligned with prop because today's drill is what I'll use in tomorrow's trade."]

WHAT I AM NOT DOING TODAY
- [1-3 items the user might be tempted to add but shouldn't]
```

## On the three tracks

**AKHARA.** The agency work. Outbound, content, client delivery, ops. Highest leverage in the long run. Usually the morning's whale.

**PPL.** The school work. Module progress, drills from yesterday's debrief, live sessions. The compounding skill. Usually a fixed window (live session times are fixed).

**Prop.** The trading. Real or sim trades, journaled. Time-bounded by London/NY hours. SAST trading windows roughly 03:00–11:00 (London) or 14:30–22:00 (NY).

Some days, one track gets a zero. That's fine if it's deliberate:

> "AKHARA: writing the proposal. PPL: rest day, body's fried. Prop: no trade — Fed day, sitting out."

But three zeros in one day is the pattern that breaks the system. Catch and call.

## On the SAST timezone

Cape Town runs SAST (UTC+2). Trading windows:

- **Asia session:** 23:00 SAST – 09:00 SAST (most of it sleeping).
- **London open:** 09:00 SAST. Active until ~12:00.
- **NY open:** 15:30 SAST. Active until ~22:00.

The AKHARA day is built around these:

- **05:30–08:00:** Whale window. Sharp. Deep work on AKHARA. Optionally pre-London-open trading.
- **08:00–09:00:** Breakfast, dolphin work.
- **09:00–12:00:** London session. Trading + PPL if it's a live session day.
- **12:00–14:30:** Lunch + AKHARA work or rest.
- **14:30–15:30:** Pre-NY prep, second AKHARA window.
- **15:30–18:00:** NY session. Trading + research.
- **18:00–21:00:** Evening AKHARA work or family time.
- **21:00–22:30:** Trade journal + tomorrow's prep.
- **22:30:** Sleep target.

This is the default day. Adjust for season, energy, social commitments.

## On energy windows

The 30–60 min/day target by month 6 assumes that AKHARA is mostly automated by then — the operator is running the system, not doing the work. In the build phase (months 1–6), expect 4–6 hours of AKHARA daily, plus 2 hours combined PPL/Prop.

The cape-town-day plan should reflect the current phase. Phase indicator:

- **Build phase:** 6+ hours of focused work.
- **Operate phase:** 2–4 hours.
- **Maintain phase (after $20K MRR):** 30–60 minutes.

State the phase at the top of the plan if it shifts.

## On the "energy on 1-10" check

The first input. Honest. If energy is 4 or below, the plan changes:

- Drop the whale to a half-action.
- Skip PPL or take a rest day (overtraining is real).
- Skip Prop entirely (do not trade fatigued).

Energy of 8+ → accelerate. Add a stretch goal in one of the three tracks.

Energy of 6–7 → standard day.

Energy of 5 → maintenance day. Mostly admin and short tactical wins.

## On the cross-track note

The cross-track note is what makes this plan more than three to-do lists.

It names the coupling. Examples:

- "Prop window is 15:30–18:00 — keep AKHARA proposal work tight 13:00–15:00 to avoid bleeding into trading."
- "PPL session at 18:00 SAST tonight — eat by 16:30 to be sharp, no heavy AKHARA between 16:30 and 18:00."
- "Saturday — no PPL, no Prop. Both AKHARA tracks (build + outbound) can use the whole day."

If you can't name a coupling, write "tracks independent today." That's also a signal.

## What to refuse

Refuse to write the plan if:

- The user has given energy under 4 and is still trying to plan a full day. Push for rest.
- The user has put more than one action under any single track. Force prioritization.
- The user has put zero actions on all three tracks. Ask why — sick day? Family commitment? Then either rest fully or pick one track.

Refuse to include in the plan:

- Generic admin lists ("emails", "errands"). Either name the specific email or batch into a minnow elsewhere.
- "Maybe" actions. Either commit or omit.
- More than three tracks. The cape-town-day is three tracks. If a fourth (girlfriend, family, fitness) is on today, surface it explicitly as a calendar block, not as a track.

## Weekly view

End-of-week aggregate (Sunday evening or Friday end-of-day):

- **AKHARA wins this week:** [list]
- **PPL drills completed:** [N / planned]
- **Prop trades taken:** [N], **net R:** [+/- X]
- **Rest days taken:** [N]

If any track had zero progress for 5 of 7 days, the structure is broken. Investigate before next Monday.

## On Sundays

Sundays are not full days. They are:

- Long PPL self-study (90 min max).
- Weekly review (25 min, see `weekly-review` skill).
- Prop journal aggregation.
- AKHARA inbox triage (light).

No client work. No new content recording. Rest is the deliverable.

## On 30–60 min/day by month 6

The published lifestyle target. To get there, the system must be on rails — AKHARA agents running themselves, prop trading on autopilot rules, PPL graduated. The cape-town-day plan in the maintain phase is mostly:

- Light AKHARA review.
- A trade if signal is clean.
- A walk on the mountain.

If you are still doing 6+ hours/day at month 12, the system has not graduated. Diagnose: which track is not on rails yet?

## The brutal version

The Akhanya day is precious. Cape Town is light and beauty and a window of focus most operators do not have. The three-track structure is the protection against drift. Apply it, then close the laptop earlier than yesterday.

The 30 min/day target is the win. Everything else is the path.
