# instagram-post

Instagram posts are not paragraphs. They are billboards. Most users see a thumbnail for 1.2 seconds, decide, and scroll. The post earns the next 1.2 seconds with the hook. It earns the swipe with the visual. It earns the save with the claim.

## The four-line shape

Every AKHARA Instagram caption is at most four lines.

**Line 1 — The hook (≤ 8 words).** A statement, not a question. Specific enough that it could not be said by another brand.

> "Three whales beat thirty minnows."

**Line 2 — The number (≤ 12 words).** A specific number that supports the hook. Revenue, time, count, percentage. Must be defensible.

> "One Sovereign client at $30K/mo replaces 50 cold leads at $2K each."

**Line 3 — The frame (≤ 15 words).** Why this matters, in one beat. The lesson, the principle, the choice.

> "Most operators are still selling hours. AKHARA sells departments."

**Line 4 — The line break and the closer (optional, ≤ 5 words).** A single phrase that lands. Often italicized via underscore. Sometimes omitted.

> "_The math, not the hustle._"

Total: under 30 words, every time. If you cannot say it in 30 words, the post is not ready — find the cuttable third of the message.

## Tags

Three tags, maximum. All branded or owned. Never use generic high-volume tags (#business, #ai, #marketing) — those are spam beacons.

Approved tag set:
- `#AKHARA`
- `#akharadepartments`
- `#commandconsole`
- `#sovereign`
- `#capetownoperator`

Pick three of the above per post. Rotate. Never use all five — looks desperate.

## Hooks that work (use as starting structures)

**The contrarian.** Take the conventional wisdom, invert it.
> "Hiring fifteen people is the slow option."

**The math.** Lead with a number.
> "$3M/year client replaces $180K/year hire."

**The rule.** A flat statement that sounds like law.
> "AKHARA does not sell hours."

**The reveal.** Set up an expectation, subvert it.
> "Most agencies grow by adding staff. We grow by removing it."

**The diagnostic.** Name a symptom the reader is feeling.
> "Your inbound is dying after 6pm."

## Hooks that fail

Never lead with:

- A question. ("Are you tired of...?") Lazy.
- An emoji. Cheap.
- "POV:" or "In this video..." Vlogger conventions; not AKHARA.
- "Did you know..." Patronizing.
- Three lines of hook before the substance. The first line is the hook.

## Visual notes (caption only — the image lives elsewhere)

Briefly note the visual that should accompany. One line. Examples:

- "Visual: still photo of the Command Console open on iPad, gold light from window."
- "Visual: white text on void background, single word `SOVEREIGN`, italic."
- "Visual: 10-second loop, slow zoom on AKHARA mark."

The visual is the hook for non-readers. Match the caption's restraint — no neon, no stock photos, no AI-generated faces.

## What never to include

- **Hashtag stuffing.** Three max.
- **"Link in bio."** Once per month at most. Becomes wallpaper otherwise.
- **"DM me to learn more."** Specific or nothing.
- **"Follow for more!"** Pleading.
- **"Tag a friend who..."** Old playbook.
- **Emojis in the body.** None. Ever.
- **Multiple line breaks ("...   ...   ...").** Padding to look longer. Cut.

## Output format

```
Caption:
[4 lines, ≤30 words]

Visual:
[1 line description]

Tags:
[3 tags, comma-separated]

Posting window:
[Best 1-hour window based on audience timezone]
```

That is the entire output.

## Cadence guidance

When the user asks "how often should we post?" — the answer:

- **3 posts/week** is the floor for momentum.
- **5 posts/week** is the ceiling for quality.
- **More than 5/week** dilutes the voice. Hold the line.

Pick 3 of: Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri. Skip weekends — the algorithm does not reward them for B2B.

## On reels vs static

Reels still outperform static for cold reach by 4x. Static outperforms for save rate. The mix:
- 60% static (the wallpaper, the principles)
- 30% reels (90-second audit Loom previews, working demos)
- 10% carousels (multi-slide longform — use script-doctor)

Avoid trends that age fast — viral audio, dance, "POV" overlays. The AKHARA frame is monastic; the format is permanent.

## The brand grid

Every post must fit one of three categories. State which when drafting:

**1. Operating doctrine.** Principles. "Three whales beat thirty minnows."
**2. Specific math.** Numbers from real engagements. Anonymized.
**3. Diagnostic.** Symptoms the reader feels, leading to a fix.

If a post does not fit one of the three, it is off-brand. Cut.

## Repurposing from the Command Console

The Command Console at `index.html` contains 30+ phrases that work as standalone posts. When stuck, mine those:

- "Three whales beat thirty minnows. Always."
- "AKHARA sells departments."
- "The cap is not capital. It is conviction."
- "Memory that does not leave when staff do."
- "Catches structural drift before it ships."

Each can anchor a 30-word post with a fresh hook line and one supporting number.

## The brutal version

Most Instagram captions are too long because the author is not confident the post is good enough on its own. Length is not depth. Cut to the bone. If the post can be screenshotted and shared without context, it has earned the bone.

Brevity is the posture. Specificity is the weapon.
