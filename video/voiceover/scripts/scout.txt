Employee zero six. The scout.

Every morning before you wake up, the scout has already read the filings, the capital raises, the LinkedIn moves, and the local market signals on every prospect on your list.

By Monday, you have a dossier. Ownership, recent decisions, soft spots, the opening line. One per account, every week.

It replaces an SDR and a researcher. The signal is twenty four hours fresh. And it never asks for a day off.
